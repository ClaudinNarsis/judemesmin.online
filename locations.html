Trains To Trichy

Trichy (Tiruchirappalli Junction · station code TPJ) is well connected from both Bangalore and Chennai. A few convenient options are listed below — please confirm live timings and availability on IRCTC before booking, as schedules can change.

From Bangalore

Both trains leave from Bangalore Cantt (BNC), not KSR Bengaluru City.
